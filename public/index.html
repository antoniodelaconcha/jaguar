Jaguar Task Scheduler

Forward scheduling on a work calendar, with resource leveling by priority. Priority comes from row position: the top row is priority 1 (most important). Drag a row up or down by its ⋮⋮ handle or priority number to change its priority (on a phone, slide the ⋮⋮ handle, or press and hold the row, then slide it). Your data is saved to your account.

Duration is in working hours. New tasks go to the bottom (lowest priority) until you drag them up. Predecessors are comma-separated ID_Item values. Fixed start is optional and means the task cannot start before that date and time. Use Columns to hide or show columns, and drag the right edge of a header to change its width (double-click the edge for the default); both are kept on this device.
Cells work like Excel: click a cell or move to it with the arrow keys and its text is selected, so typing replaces it. F2 or a second click edits inside the text. Enter moves down, Tab moves right, Esc undoes the edit. Dates and times show as yymmmdd HH:MM on a 24-hour clock (26Nov20 14:30). Type them that way, as 2026-11-20 14:30 or as 20/11/2026 14:30, or pick them with the calendar button in the cell (or Alt+Down). If you type only a date, the time is a default from the schedule, shown in grey and not saved: for Fixed start it is the predecessor's finish (after leveling) if that falls on the same day, otherwise the start of that working day; this follows the calendar and its overrides. For Pdd (probable due date) it is noon (12:00); a task finishing after its Pdd is late.
Waiting = working hours a task was ready (predecessors done, fixed start passed) but its resource was working on something with higher priority.
