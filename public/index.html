Jaguar Task Scheduler

Forward scheduling on a work calendar, with resource leveling by priority. Priority comes from row position: the top row is priority 1 (most important). Drag a row by its ⋮⋮ handle, or use ▲ ▼, to change its priority. Your data is saved to your account.
